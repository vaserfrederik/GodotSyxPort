DESC_LONG:
"Garthimi were created by the fallen god Aminion in the first war of the gods. After his imprisonment, the Garthimi retreated to the mountain caverns of the world from which they raided the ones foolish enough to settle nearby. They reproduce quickly and their exoskeleton is quite resistant to all kinds of weather, while also providing them with a natural battle boost. 
Despite their violent nature, they are very lawful and place great importance on the well-being of their hive as a whole. Ordinarily, they would never harm their hive or queen, but great stress can cause them to revert to a more primal and even more vicious state.
Garthimi are good miners and do not mind working in the more hazardous mines. They are the only species that can make use of Balticrawlers, which have a symbiotic relationship with them. They lack dextrous hands, severely limiting their usefulness in most industries. Despite that limitation, they make excellent stonecutters due to being able to mold rock like clay after dousing it in their secretions.
Ambush predators at heart, they require a lot of training to be useful on the battlefield, but well-trained Garthimi make excellent infantry.

They like Fish and Meat.
